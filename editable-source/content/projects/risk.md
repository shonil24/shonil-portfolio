## Why this mattered
Operations teams needed portfolio visibility without exposing every audience to every record.

## Approach
Translate stakeholder questions into a governed Power BI model with role-level access and pricing-focused KPI views.

## Trade-offs and reasoning
Governance is part of the product, not a final permission step. This keeps access safer and definitions consistent.

## Outcome
A shared decision tool supports ad-hoc analysis across finance, sales and operations.
