## Why this mattered
Public pandemic data was available at scale, but raw counts were difficult to interpret without context and visual comparison.

## Approach
Clean and structure the data, then use statistical summaries and interactive views to surface patterns across time and geography.

## Trade-offs and reasoning
The project prioritises clarity and accessibility over an encyclopedic dashboard. Fewer, stronger visual stories make the output easier to use.

## Outcome
A practical visual analysis helps people explore change rather than stare at isolated numbers.
