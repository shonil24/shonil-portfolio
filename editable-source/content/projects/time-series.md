## Why this mattered
Forecasts are only useful when their assumptions and limitations are visible.

## Approach
Explore time-based patterns, build candidate models and evaluate them against held-out observations.

## Trade-offs and reasoning
Interpretability and evaluation discipline matter as much as model complexity.

## Outcome
A structured approach turns historical patterns into useful planning signals.
