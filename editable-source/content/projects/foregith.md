## Why this mattered
Sales and operations teams needed a fast way to see what was changing, where revenue was coming from, and which segments needed attention.

## Approach
Shape the data around decisions first, then build a focused KPI layer with drill-downs for channel, product class and period. Forecasting adds a forward-looking view instead of only reporting yesterday.

## Trade-offs and reasoning
A smaller set of decision-ready measures is more useful than a dashboard that tries to answer every possible question. Detail stays available through drill-down rather than competing with the headline.

## Outcome
A governed, self-serve view supports faster commercial conversations and more consistent reporting.
